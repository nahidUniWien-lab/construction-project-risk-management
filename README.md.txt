# Construction Project Risk Management – Business Analysis Case Study

## Project Overview

This project is a business analysis and data visualization case study focused on construction project risk management.

The objective is to help project managers and management identify projects with cost overruns, schedule delays, open risks, and high overall risk levels.

The project demonstrates the complete process from identifying a business problem to defining requirements, developing user stories, prioritising features, creating a Power BI dashboard, and performing user acceptance testing.

---

## Business Problem

Construction projects can experience cost overruns, schedule delays, and multiple open risks.

Management needs a simple and structured way to identify high-risk projects early and focus attention on projects that require corrective action.

## Business Analysis

### Requirements

Functional and non-functional requirements were defined to translate the business problem into system requirements.

### Process Modelling

An AS-IS and TO-BE process was created to demonstrate how a digital risk-monitoring solution could improve manual project monitoring.

### User Stories

User stories were developed using:

As a [user], I want [function], so that [business benefit].

### Product Backlog

Features were prioritised according to their expected business value and importance.

### Acceptance Testing

Manual acceptance tests were performed to verify dashboard functionality including project visibility, cost calculations, risk filtering, and location filtering.

## Key Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify projects that require management attention based on:

- Cost overruns
- Schedule delays
- Number of open risks
- Overall risk classification

Projects experiencing both significant cost overruns and schedule delays can be prioritised for further management review.
